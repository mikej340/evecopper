The Designer

Eve Copper

Eve Copper is a designer working at the intersection of craft and utility. Each piece is made in limited runs with considered construction — garments intended to move with the wearer rather than constrain them.

The multiwear approach was born from a desire to resist disposability. A single garment that reconfigures its silhouette is both a practical and philosophical proposition — fewer pieces, worn more often, worn differently.

Made in Runs of 30

Each piece is produced in a limited run of thirty. No restocks. Once it's gone, it's gone.

Considered Materials

Natural fibres, UK-sourced where possible. Every fabric is chosen for how it moves and how it lasts.

Worn Multiple Ways

Every garment is designed to configure itself differently. The care label includes instructions for each look.
